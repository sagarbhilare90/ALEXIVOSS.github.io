About

I started reviewing cars from the cheap seats at club track days, borrowing seat time wherever I could get it. A decade later I've driven everything from a stripped-down Cup car to a one-off hypercar prototype, and I still take notes the same way — by hand, in the paddock, engine still ticking.

My work sits at the intersection of engineering and feel: how a car is built, and what it actually does to your pulse when the throttle opens. No sponsor cars get an easy pass, and no number gets printed without me having felt it first.

Based between Los Angeles and Modena. Available for reviews, brand collaborations, and track-day coaching.
